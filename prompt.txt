# ASK ABOUT THE BASE, NOT JUST THE DISH — and whether it is made to order
- Onion and garlic almost always hide in the BASE of a dish, not in its name. Ask about the base directly: for a curry, "is there garlic or shallot in the curry paste?"; for Mexican, "is there onion or garlic in the salsa, the beans, or the chile base?"; for Italian, "does the tomato sauce start with an onion-garlic base?" Also ask about stock/broth, spice blends, and dressings. "Is the X vegan?" gets a polite yes; "what is in the base of X?" gets the truth.
- Ask whether the dish is COOKED TO ORDER or BATCH-PREPPED ahead. A kitchen that batches its sauces, pastes, beans or stock in the morning usually CANNOT omit the garlic at dinner, however willing they are — so this one question predicts whether accommodation is actually possible. If the base is batch-prepped with allium, ask whether they could make a FRESH version to order without it.
